On-device processing

QingMenu performs file operations on your Mac. It does not send file contents, filenames, paths, clipboard contents, or activity history to the developer. The app does not require an account and contains no advertising or third-party analytics.

Files and the clipboard

When you select a folder and grant access, QingMenu saves a permission bookmark on your Mac so it can use the folder again. Imported templates and icons, favorite folders, menu preferences, and pending commands from Finder are also stored locally.

QingMenu remembers your favorite apps on your Mac. When you choose Open with Favorite App, macOS passes the selected files or folder to that app. You can remove the entry in Favorite Apps.

When you open the context menu, QingMenu checks which types of content the clipboard offers to decide which commands to show. When you choose to save clipboard text or an image, the app reads that content and writes a file to the folder you chose in Finder. Pasting files reads the list of files to process. Copying names, paths, or checksums and cutting files write to the system clipboard.

Managing your data

In General settings, you can forget saved folder permissions. You can remove imported templates, icons, and favorite folders in their respective settings sections. macOS manages the clipboard. Copy another item to replace its contents. Files you create, copy, or move using QingMenu stay under your control.

Trial and Purchases

Apple processes trials, purchases, renewals and refunds. QingMenu verifies transactions and access periods on the device with StoreKit. The developer does not receive your payment card details through the app.
